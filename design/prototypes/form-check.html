What it checks on a squat

DepthKnee angle must reach about 75° or less with the hips at knee level. Shallow reps are not counted.
Back angleTorso lean over 60° from vertical triggers a "chest up" cue.
TempoTime down and up is measured so controlled reps score higher.

In the app this runs on the phone with on-device pose estimation, so video never leaves the device. Feedback is coaching guidance with a confidence level shown; it does not diagnose injuries.
